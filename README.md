# Hangout Timer

A hang timer for grip and strength training, with reaction-delay correction.

**Live:** https://imraansafla.github.io/hangout-timer/

- Countdown of 10 or 5 seconds with beeps on 3, 2, 1 and a "go" beep the moment the timer starts.
- Live hang timer with a milestone animation every 30 seconds.
- After you drop, tell it how long you took to tap STOP, and it shows your adjusted hang time.
- Tag the hand(s) you used; recent sets are listed on the home screen.
- Tap the hanging figure on the home screen. Tap her three times quickly for a pull-up.

A single self-contained HTML file, with no build step and no dependencies.
